# 🔥 Campfire

**Less scrolling. More stories.**

Campfire is a cross-platform social app designed to get people off their phones and interacting with the people around them.

Instead of filling downtime with more screen time, Campfire gives groups games, questions, challenges, and conversation prompts designed to create actual human interaction.

The phone starts the experience.

Then the goal is to put it down.

---

## The Idea

Waiting in line. Sitting at a restaurant. Killing time at an airport. Hanging out with friends.

We already have entertainment in our pockets, but that often means everyone ends up staring at a different screen.

Campfire takes a different approach.

1. Start or join a Campfire.
2. Gather your group.
3. Get a challenge, game, or prompt.
4. Put the phones down.
5. Actually interact.

Campfire is intentionally designed so that successful use of the app means spending **less time using the app**.

## Design Philosophy

Campfire has one unusual design constraint:

> **The better the app works, the less you use it.**

Every feature should encourage interaction between people rather than interaction with the screen.

The interface is intentionally simple, warm, and unobtrusive.

No endless feeds.

No reason to scroll.

No digital world to disappear into.

Just enough technology to start something in the real one.
